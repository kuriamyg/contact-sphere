# 06 — Data subject requests (DPA s.26; General Regulations 2021)

## Who can ask

- **Users**, about their own data — most rights are self-service in the app.
- **People in a user's contacts** — they are not our users; see "Contacts' requests"
  below.

## Deadlines (Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021)

| Right                   | How                                                                                                                          | Deadline                            |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Access (reg. 9)         | Self-service: everything is visible in the app; Profile → Export contacts (.vcf). By email: send a copy of the account data. | **7 days**                          |
| Rectification (reg. 10) | Self-service editing; by email for account details                                                                           | **14 days**                         |
| Erasure (reg. 12)       | Profile → Delete your account (immediate); by email if the user cannot sign in, after verifying identity                     | **14 days**                         |
| Portability (reg. 11)   | Profile → Export contacts (.vcf) — a standard, machine-readable format                                                       | **30 days**                         |
| Objection / restriction | By email; stop the processing unless there is an overriding legal reason                                                     | As soon as possible, within 14 days |

## Handling an email request

1. Log it privately: date received, who, what right, deadline.
2. **Verify identity** before acting: the request must come from the account's email
   address, or the person must sign in. Never reveal or delete data for an unverified
   requester.
3. Act, and reply in writing, in the person's language where possible.
4. If refusing (for example the request is manifestly unfounded), explain why and
   mention the right to complain to the ODPC.
5. No fee for access, correction or erasure.

## Contacts' requests

Contacts' details belong to the user who stored them, and Contact Sphere cannot see or
search across users. If someone asks "do you hold my data?":

1. Explain that each user's address book is private to that user, and that we do not
   look inside it.
2. Suggest they ask the person they believe stored their details to correct or remove
   them.
3. If they give a specific account (for example the user's email) and a strong reason
   (harassment, unlawful processing), investigate with the account holder, and act
   under the terms of use if they are broken.

## Records

Keep the request log privately (not in this repository) for at least two years.
